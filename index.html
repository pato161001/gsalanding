We’re Gray Spark Audio, a recording studio in Pune specializing in the Art of Sound.

Established in 2011 by Ronak Runwal, Gray Spark is a 5000 sq. ft. music production and audio post-production facility, just a couple of hours from Mumbai. Our team of acclaimed engineers works across ads, films, scores, jingles, voice-overs, indie music, and more.

Home to six state-of-the-art studios — six dedicated studio spaces. We serve both indie artists and commercial producers. At Gray Spark, we believe in turnkey solutions — delivering convenience and quality under one roof.
