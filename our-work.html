netflix — sacred games

The show that pressed record on prestige TV in India.

Location recording, ADR and post — cut inside the live room while the sirens still rang in our ears.

supercell — clash of clans

A game score played by 25 humans, at once.

Recording, production and mix for a global spot — the live room finally at full capacity.

Four short films. One anthology of small, loud silences.

Voice, foley and score — printed here, mixed here, held to the frame here.

The kind of record you fall asleep to on repeat.

Mix and master. Wide, warm, unhurried — the way a ballad wants to sit in a room.

A Bombay rap story that lived or died by its sound.

Dialogue, sync sound and a score built from the streets — we would have mixed this one loud.

a. r. rahman — firdaus studios

A scoring stage built for the 21st century.

Studio tour, tracking and mix insight from one of the most recorded composers in the world — the benchmark we measure ourselves against.
