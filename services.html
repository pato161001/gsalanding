02 / What we do

Studio
services.

idea to finished work →

Gray Spark Audio brings together the people, spaces and expertise to take your project from idea to finished work. We work with artists, filmmakers and brands across music recording, music production, voice-overs, film music and sound design — with experienced engineers, producers and composers guiding every project. Our studios support Dolby Atmos 7.1.4, 7.1 and 5.1 mixing for immersive, platform-ready audio.

01

Recording

From full albums and EPs to live multitrack recordings, we offer the space, gear and people to bring your music to life. Our expansive backline and roster of session musicians help fill any gaps. All six studios are interconnected, allowing bands to record live while keeping every instrument cleanly isolated.

See our studios →
02

Music Production

Whether you're starting with just a set of lyrics or a fully formed idea, we help shape it into a finished track. We work with solo artists, composers and bands, from developing arrangements and sounds to producing complete EPs and albums, bringing the right creative and technical expertise to every project.

More on music production →
03

Mix and Master

Our flagship mix and master space is built for precision, with Dolby Atmos 7.1.4 monitoring and a Neumann-designed monitoring setup. As a Neumann partner, we bring world-class monitoring and immersive audio expertise to every project, from detailed stereo mixes to fully immersive Dolby Atmos productions.

Book a session →
04

Film Music and Sound Design

Our exceptional group of sound designers, location sound engineers, background score composers, foley artists and mixing engineers, combined with our state-of-the-art facilities, produce sound that meets international standards. We strike a balance between creativity and technical expertise, selecting the talent that aligns with your project's needs.

More on film sound →
05

Ad Music and Sound Design

Gray Spark Audio has extensive experience crafting music and sound that elevates stories for some of the world's leading brands. From powerful campaigns for SBI and Samsung, to global sports anthems for the ICC World Cup, and dynamic soundtracks for KIA, our work blends creativity with precision — audio that complements visuals and resonates with audiences worldwide.
